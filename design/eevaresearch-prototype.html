Methodology

How signals are found, labelled, and checked

Where the material comes from

Signals are extracted from primary filings across five venues — EDGAR, TDnet, DART, CNINFO, and HKEX — rather than from press coverage. A large share of what matters appears first in a footnote, a segment note, or a Japanese- or Korean-language disclosure that English coverage does not pick up for days.

The four labels

  • Fact — stated in a source document, and linked to it. No link, no label.
  • Interpretation — a market read built on facts shown alongside it.
  • Inference — follows logically from the evidence but is not confirmed anywhere.
  • Uncertainty — a named open question. Recorded rather than smoothed over.

Novelty check

Before a signal is published it is checked against prior coverage by source and date. If the material has already been reported, it is not a signal.

Contrary evidence

Every signal carries a contrary-evidence field. When none has been recorded, the drawer says so explicitly rather than hiding the section — an absent counter-argument and an unexamined one are different things.

About

What the tool does and where its data comes from

What the tool does

EevaResearch tracks the companies, supply chains, catalysts, bottlenecks, and capital flows behind five technology investment themes. The premise is that major trends create opportunity across an entire supply chain, not only in the largest headline companies — so the work is figuring out which layer benefits, where the bottleneck actually sits, and who has direct versus second-order exposure.

Five themes

  • AI Buildout — compute, data centers, networking, power, cooling, packaging, servers
  • Humanoids — actuators, motion systems, sensors, machine vision, edge compute, batteries
  • Space — launch, satellites, satellite communications, Earth observation, defense
  • Memory — DRAM, HBM, NAND, controllers, equipment, materials, testing, packaging
  • Photonics — optical transceivers, lasers, silicon photonics, coherent networking, interconnect

Data sources

SEC EDGAR, TDnet (Japan), DART (Korea), CNINFO (China), and HKEXnews, plus market pricing for breadth and rotation calculations. Filing coverage varies by venue; pricing is delayed.

JX Advanced Metals outlines ¥120B InP substrate capacity program

Direction
Improving
Strength
High
Horizon

What changed

A capacity commitment of this size at the substrate layer implies the constraint sits upstream of transceiver assembly, not at it.

Evidence
当社は、インジウムリン基板の生産能力拡大に向け、約1,200億円の投資を計画しております。The Company plans to invest approximately ¥120 billion toward expanding production capacity for indium phosphide substrates.JX Advanced Metals · TDnet · 14 Aug 2026 · p.7
Contrary evidence

The figure spans a multi-year program with no annual breakdown, and part may fund maintenance rather than net new capacity. Two peers announced expansions in the same quarter, so incremental industry supply may be smaller than the headline suggests.

Validates if

Customer allocation is disclosed, or a downstream buyer confirms a long-term supply agreement at the substrate layer.

Invalidates if

Capex guidance is revised down, or the program is restated as maintenance in the next quarterly report.
