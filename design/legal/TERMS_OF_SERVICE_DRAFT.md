## 1. What EevaResearch is

EevaResearch is an evidence-first filing-intelligence tool. It ingests
regulatory filings from the United States (SEC EDGAR), South Korea
(OpenDART) and Japan (EDINET), together with curated news feeds; detects
material disclosures against a typed theme taxonomy; extracts and
translates supporting text; and presents the result as reviewable
signals, research cases and a daily digest.
